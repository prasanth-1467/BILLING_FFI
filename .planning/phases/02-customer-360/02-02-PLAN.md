# Plan 02-02: Customer 360 Drawer, Financial Ledger & Direct Actions

## Objective
Build the Customer 360 side drawer displaying a real-time financial ledger, transaction history, quotation pipeline, and quick communication actions (WhatsApp, Phone call, Email). Integrate the drawer into the Customer Directory table.

## Context
Currently, `Customers.jsx` displays basic contact information in a table with simple edit/delete buttons. Users cannot view an account's financial standing, outstanding invoices, or previous quotations without manually switching between Invoices and Quotations pages.

## Tasks
1. **Create Customer 360 Drawer (`Customer360Drawer.jsx`)**:
   - Header with customer name, lifecycle pill (`StatusBadge`), GSTIN badge, category tags, and quick-action buttons (WhatsApp direct chat, Phone dialer, Email composer).
   - Financial Metric Ribbon: Total Billed, Total Paid, Outstanding Balance, Overdue Amount, Credit Limit meter.
   - Tabbed Interface:
     - **Tab 1: Financial Ledger / Invoices**: Chronological list of invoices with status, invoice number, date, amount, balance, and PDF download action.
     - **Tab 2: Quotations & Pipeline**: History of quotations with conversion statuses (`Draft`, `Sent`, `Approved`, `Converted`).
     - **Tab 3: Customer Details & Notes**: Editable notes, payment terms, credit limits, and full address.
2. **Integrate into `Customers.jsx`**:
   - Add "View 360" eye action button and clickable row to open the 360 Drawer.
   - Display outstanding balance pill and lifecycle stage directly in the customer table for rapid at-a-glance scanning.
   - Add new optional fields (Email, Credit Limit, Payment Terms, Lifecycle Stage, Notes) to the Customer create/edit modal.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Clicking a customer row or "View 360" button smoothly slides open the 360 Drawer.
- [ ] Financial metrics, invoices, and quotations load seamlessly from `/api/customers/:id/summary`.
- [ ] WhatsApp link generates pre-formatted payment inquiry or account statement greeting.
- [ ] Existing customer creation and edit flows work without validation issues.
