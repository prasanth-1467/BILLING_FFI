# Research: CRM Capabilities for Agricultural & Industrial Irrigation

## 1. Domain Persona Analysis
Fine Flow Irrigation serves four distinct customer segments:
1. **Farmers**: Focus on government subsidy documentation, crop seasons, localized delivery, direct cash/UPI or delayed harvest payments.
2. **Dealers**: High-volume, recurring purchases, tiered discounts, strict credit limits, outstanding ledger reconciliation.
3. **Contractors**: Project-based quotations, milestone payments, equipment warranties, and site-specific deliveries.
4. **Institutions / Corporate**: Formal GST invoices, purchase order cross-referencing, multi-tier approvals, compliance documents.

## 2. Core CRM Feature Matrix

### Phase 1: Customer 360 & Ledger View
- Instant summary of customer lifetime revenue, total invoices, pending balances, and quotation conversion rate.
- Consolidated transaction timeline: chronologically lists quotations, invoices, payments, and credit notes.
- Quick contact actions: Click-to-call / WhatsApp trigger with pre-filled payment reminder or quotation link.

### Phase 2: Quotation Pipeline & Follow-up Tracking
- Visual pipeline/kanban or status tags for quotations: Draft → Sent → Negotiation → Accepted → Invoiced / Lost.
- Follow-up date scheduling with automated agent reminders (`InvoicerAgent`).

### Phase 3: Outstanding Aging & Credit Monitoring
- Receivables aging bucket breakdown per customer.
- Credit limit alerts during invoice creation if pending balance exceeds configured limit.

### Phase 4: Interaction Logs & Notes
- Simple interaction logging (Call, Meeting, Site Visit, WhatsApp) with timestamp and next action date.
