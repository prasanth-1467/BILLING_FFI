# Roadmap: Milestone 1 — Modern CRM & UI/UX Transformation

## Milestone Goal
Transform Fine Flow Irrigation's GST billing platform into an integrated Billing + CRM command center, dramatically elevating frontend UI/UX and customer visibility while strictly preserving all existing GST, invoicing, and PDF functionality.

---

## Phase Breakdown

### Phase 1: UI/UX Modernization & Navigation Baseline [COMPLETE]
- **Goal**: Modernize visual hierarchy, navigation sidebar, top bars, and table layouts across the application for high ergonomics and consistency.
- **Scope**:
  - Refine Tailwind theme tokens and layout structure in `billing-frontend`.
  - Polish sidebar navigation, user profile header, and notification drawer triggers.
  - Standardize loading skeletons, empty states, and error handling components.
  - Zero changes to backend APIs or core business calculations.
- **Success Criteria**: Clean, modern responsive design verified on desktop and mobile viewport widths without breaking existing routes or features.

### Phase 2: Executive CRM Dashboard & Receivables Aging [COMPLETE]
- **Goal**: Re-engineer the main dashboard into an actionable executive CRM overview.
- **Scope**:
  - Financial KPI Cards: Monthly Revenue, Outstanding Receivables, Critical Overdue (>30 / >60 days), Active Pipeline.
  - Receivables Aging analysis widget (0–30, 31–60, 60+ days) with one-click reminder shortcuts.
  - Quotation Conversion Funnel visualization (Draft → Sent → Approved → Invoiced).
  - Integrated notification feed surfacing alerts generated by `InsightAgent`, `InvoicerAgent`, and `RestockAgent`.
- **Success Criteria**: Dashboard presents instant clarity on cashflow, overdue risks, and quote conversion rates without performance degradation.

### Phase 3: Quotation Follow-up Workflow & Conversion Pipeline [ACTIVE]
- **Goal**: Empower sales and billing staff to track quotation follow-ups, schedule reminder dates, record customer feedback notes, and trigger direct WhatsApp messages.
- **Scope**:
  - Additive non-breaking fields to `Quotation` model (`followUpDate`, `followUpNotes`, `followUpStatus`).
  - Backend endpoint `PATCH /api/quotations/:id/follow-up`.
  - Quotations table enhancements: Follow-up status badges, "Due Today" / "Overdue" indicators, and quick filter tabs.
  - Schedule Follow-up modal with date picker, status selector, and notes textarea.
  - One-click personalized WhatsApp quotation follow-up message trigger.
- **Success Criteria**: Sales staff can track every proposal through negotiation to invoice conversion with zero lost deals.

### Phase 4: Customer 360 & Financial Ledger
- **Goal**: Upgrade customer management from simple contact entries into a comprehensive 360° customer profile with financial ledger visibility.
- **Scope**:
  - Non-breaking additive extensions to `Customer` model (credit limit, payment terms, lifecycle stage, notes).
  - Backend endpoint `/api/customers/:id/summary` aggregating invoices, quotations, total billed, paid, and balance due.
  - Modern Customer 360 drawer/view with tabbed ledger (Invoices, Quotations, Profile).
  - Quick WhatsApp and Phone communication triggers.
- **Success Criteria**: Customer profile instantly displays full financial status and transaction history with 100% preservation of existing customer create/edit flows.
